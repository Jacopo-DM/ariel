Spec (Claude, 2026-10-19; the throwaway prototype is in the session scratchpad and was seen and corrected by the user five times; the sample body was changed from the flat gecko to a hand-built showcase with modules on TOP and BOTTOM, because top and bottom looked identical on a flat robot):

Tick labels are world coordinates; a flipped axis is labelled "y (m), flipped". The gizmo is a small inset above the top left of each panel, outside the plot area; the perspective view carries an x, y, z tripod outside the robot's box. The perspective view uses `Poly3DCollection`, a light factor per face normal (outward normals), limits that are cubes of one side so that proportions are true, and `bbox_inches="tight"` when saved.

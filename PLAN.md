Spec (Claude, 2026-10-19; the throwaway prototype is in the session scratchpad and was seen and corrected by the user five times; the sample body was changed from the flat gecko to a hand-built showcase with modules on TOP and BOTTOM, because top and bottom looked identical on a flat robot):

- Views (the front of the robot is +x, up is +z): top has the front up and the robot's right on the right (horizontal axis -y, vertical x); bottom (y, x); left (-x, z); right (x, z); front (y, z); back (-y, z). Faces are sorted far to near along the view direction. Depth shading in the projections (asked by the user 2026-10-19: orthographic views show every box with the same face, so depth is invisible): each face is darkened by its distance from the viewer (`DEPTH_SHADE` 0.35 at the farthest face, none at the nearest) and by the light factor (0.8 plus 0.2 times the light), and faces turned away from the viewer are not drawn. The outward normal of a face is taken relative to the centre of its own box, not of the whole robot (the first prototype used the robot's centre and dropped boxes in the left view). Tick labels are world coordinates; a flipped axis is labelled "y (m), flipped". The gizmo is a small inset above the top left of each panel, outside the plot area; the perspective view carries an x, y, z tripod outside the robot's box.

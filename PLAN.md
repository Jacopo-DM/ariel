### Goal robot-pictures: approved 2026-10-19 (goal and proposals (a) to (d) approved; spec and tasks wait for approval)

Decisions, from the user (2026-10-19): `plot_robot(robot, views=("top","bottom","left","right","front","back","perspective"), *, grid=True, gizmo=True, elevation=30.0, azimuth=-60.0)` returns a matplotlib `Figure`, no OpenGL, **built robots only** (a `CoreModule`; the caller builds a graph first, so the picture is of the body that was simulated); it lives in `src/ariel/visualisation/` with a row in `tests/functional/README.md`, a functional notebook and a short docs page whose figures are generated at docs build time. The saver is an opt-in `EAOperation`, **three booleans, no "all"**: `save_pictures(to_robot, *, folder, is_maximisation=True, snap_best=False, snap_median=False, snap_worst=False, views=...)`; median, not mean (a mean is no individual). **Two files per picked individual**: `<folder>/gen_NNNN/<rank>_projections.png` (top, bottom, left, right, front, back as six panels, 2 columns by 3 rows, opposite views side by side) and `<rank>_perspective.png`, `<rank>` being best, median or worst. Every view has a coordinate gizmo and a metre grid, each switchable. The user approved proposals (a) to (d): the operation gets `is_maximisation` and `folder` as arguments; pictures are drawn with `matplotlib.figure.Figure`, never `pyplot`, in the main process (an `EAOperation` runs after the worker pool has returned), each figure released after saving; the docs figures come from matplotlib's `plot` directive; default elevation 30 and azimuth -60.

Spec (Claude, 2026-10-19; the throwaway prototype is in the session scratchpad and was seen and corrected by the user five times; the sample body was changed from the flat gecko to a hand-built showcase with modules on TOP and BOTTOM, because top and bottom looked identical on a flat robot):
- Geometry: compile `robot.spec`, `mj_forward` (joint angles zero, as the overlap check), every geom except planes becomes its bounding box (`half_extent` of `tests/functional/prebuilt_robots.ipynb`: sphere, capsule, cylinder, mesh by their size or `geom_aabb`, box and ellipsoid by `geom_size`), drawn as six faces in the geom's colour. Derived from the robot: every colour (`geom_rgba`, set by the module classes: brick blue, hinge red, core yellow), every position and size (`geom_xpos`, `geom_xmat`, `geom_size`), the view limits (the box of the drawing, centred, the same half span in both directions plus 8% of the longest side), the tick step (a round number near a fifth of the longest side, minor lines at a fifth of it, half of it on the perspective view) and the gizmo size (16% of the longest side). Constants: the figure size per panel, line widths, grid greys, the light direction (0.4, -0.5, 0.8), its ambient part 0.55 (0.8 in the projections), `DEPTH_SHADE`, the perspective focal length 2.5 and box zoom 1.05, the view conventions below. The constants are named, in one place.
- Views (the front of the robot is +x, up is +z): top has the front up and the robot's right on the right (horizontal axis -y, vertical x); bottom (y, x); left (-x, z); right (x, z); front (y, z); back (-y, z). Faces are sorted far to near along the view direction. Depth shading in the projections (asked by the user 2026-10-19: orthographic views show every box with the same face, so depth is invisible): each face is darkened by its distance from the viewer (`DEPTH_SHADE` 0.35 at the farthest face, none at the nearest) and by the light factor (0.8 plus 0.2 times the light), and faces turned away from the viewer are not drawn. The outward normal of a face is taken relative to the centre of its own box, not of the whole robot (the first prototype used the robot's centre and dropped boxes in the left view). Tick labels are world coordinates; a flipped axis is labelled "y (m), flipped". The gizmo is a small inset above the top left of each panel, outside the plot area; the perspective view carries an x, y, z tripod outside the robot's box. The perspective view uses `Poly3DCollection`, a light factor per face normal (outward normals), limits that are cubes of one side so that proportions are true, and `bbox_inches="tight"` when saved.
- `plot_robot` with the default views returns one figure with 2 columns, the perspective panel in the last row; `save_pictures` calls it twice, once for the six projections and once for the perspective view.
- `save_pictures` picks among the alive individuals that have a fitness (the others are skipped), ranked like `EA.get_solution` (descending when `is_maximisation`, ascending otherwise; `Population.sort` is a stable `sorted`, so ties keep the population's order; median is the item at index `len // 2`, worst the last). It calls `to_robot(individual)`, the same function the evaluation uses, so a decode that repairs overlaps is drawn repaired. The generation number comes from the population: the largest `time_of_death` of its individuals, which the EA sets to `current_generation` on every commit (checked in V3, because the operation runs before that commit and the last generation may lag by one). With all three toggles off it writes nothing and returns the population unchanged; it never changes the population.

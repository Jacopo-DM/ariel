overlap¶

Find modules of a robot body that sit inside each other.

The body is built and compiled, every geom becomes a Trimesh box, and python-fcl (through trimesh.collision) reports the penetration depth of every colliding pair. Two modules overlap when their depth is more than TOLERANCE times the thinnest side of the smaller of the two, so the rule does not depend on the size of the robot. Parent and child modules share a face on purpose and are never compared.

Notes

  • A module is a graph node. Its geoms are named

    <parent>-<node>-<face>-<kind>; the core’s geom is core.

  • The size of a module is the total volume of its geoms; its thinnest

    side is twice the smallest half-size among them.

  • Poses are at joint angle zero, as compiled.

  • The later module of an overlapping pair is the deeper one in the tree;

    at equal depth, the one whose path from the core takes the later face (ModuleFaces order) at the first edge where the two paths differ. The repair removes it with everything attached beyond it.

box_mesh(extents: tuple[float, float, float]) → trimesh.Trimesh¶

Make the Trimesh box of a size, once.

Trimesh tests every mesh for convexity when it is added to a collision manager and keeps the answer on the mesh, so reusing one mesh for every box of a size removes most of the time of a collision check.

Parameters:

extents (tuple[float, float, float]) – The full side lengths.

Returns:

The box, centred on the origin. It is shared: do not change it.

repair_overlaps(graph: Graph, build: Builder) → Graph¶

Prune modules until no two modules overlap beyond the tolerance.

Each round builds the body, finds the overlapping pairs, and removes the later module of the pair whose later module is deepest (ties: the smaller node id), with its subtree; removing the deepest first keeps the most of the body. Every round removes at least one module, so the loop ends.

Parameters:

  • graph (nx.DiGraph[int]) – The robot graph; it is not changed.

  • build (Callable) – Builds the body of a graph, for example the constructor.

Returns:

A copy of the graph without overlaps; the graph itself when there were none.

Return type:

nx.DiGraph[int]

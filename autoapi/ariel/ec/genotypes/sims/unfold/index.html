unfold¶

Unfold a Sims genome into the body graph the builder takes.

Unfolding starts at the core and grows the body in rounds: in each round every module placed in the round before grows the children its template’s connections ask for, in the order the connections are listed. A template may appear at most its copy limit times along one path from the core, and never more than a fifth of the maximum number of modules. A connection marked terminal_only grows only from the last copy of a chain. A face that already holds a module keeps it: the first connection wins.

Mirroring. A module whose mirror image (through the body’s centre plane, the plane through the core from front to back) is the module itself lies on that plane: the core, and chains grown straight along it. A connection with reflect set, from such a module, also grows a mirrored twin of its child on the opposite face, and the twin’s whole subtree is the mirror image of the child’s. An original and its twin are placed together or not at all, so a symmetric genotype stays symmetric when the maximum number of modules cuts it short.

The faces and tilts of a mirrored module are found from the real module frames: each placed module carries the reflection, in its own coordinates, that maps it onto its mirror image, and the child’s face and tilt are the ones whose site and frame match that reflection (DESIGN.md Decisions, “Mirroring modules”). A brick keeps its tilt; a hinge at 45 degrees becomes one at -45.

Find the mirror image of a child on face of a mirrored parent.

Parameters:

  • parent – Type of the parent module.

  • parent_mirror – Reflection that maps the parent onto its mirror image, in the parent’s frame.

  • face – Face of the parent the original child grows on.

  • tilt – ModuleRotationsTheta name of the original child’s tilt.

  • child – Type of the child module.

Returns:

The face and tilt of the mirror image on the mirrored parent, and its own reflection.

Return type:

Placement

Raises:

ValueError – If no face and tilt give the mirror image. Every reflection that unfolding passes maps the parent’s faces onto faces it has, so this means the module frames changed under it.

Parameters:
